# Prospect calls — Fri Sep 25 (Nadia)

Two 20-min demand interviews, recruited from the waitlist. Showed the clickable prototype at the end of each. My notes, not a transcript.

## Call 1 — Rachel K.
- 38, divorced twice (her words), "done with the apps"
- How she meets people now: friends set her up, "but it's random and awkward"
- When I explained TeAmour: "oh so it's like my friends setting me up but organized?" — got it fast
- Pricing: said she'd pay ~$10/mo "if my friends actually showed up for it"
- Big doubt: would her friends really do this. "They have kids, jobs. Nobody's going to fill out a questionnaire for me." Came back to this twice.
- Prototype: tapped around the team screen looking for how to add her friend, ended up in settings. Found the + after a nudge from me.
- Liked the idea that a friend has to vouch — "that's the safety thing for me"

## Call 2 — Jordan
- Uses Hinge, "it's fine"
- Didn't get what was different from Hinge until I walked him through the wingman part. Landing page headline "Date with your friends' help" didn't land for him — "sounds like a group chat"
- I pitched the Scout agent for ~3 min. He said "yeah, sounds cool, I'd try it." (no follow-up questions)
- Would want to see who on his team suggested a match — "otherwise it's just another algorithm"
- Asked whether his friends need the app too or can do it by text
- Prototype: no issues finding things, said the match card "makes sense"

## Waitlist message test (this week)
- Variant B ("Your friends know your type") is converting better than A so far — too early to call, ~60 signups each
- (my take) we should lead with the vouching/safety angle in onboarding — haven't tested it yet
